Why folk come back

Three generations of the same family have been frying on this corner. We keep it simple: good fish, good tatties, and a fryer that’s never left to idle.

Fresh haddock daily

Landed at Peterhead and on our counter the same morning. Never frozen.

Beef dripping

The old way, for a crisper batter. Veggie items go in a separate vegetable-oil fryer.

Angus tatties

Maris Pipers from a farm up the road, hand-cut every afternoon.

Bridies & pehs

From a Dundee bakery we’ve used for over thirty years.
